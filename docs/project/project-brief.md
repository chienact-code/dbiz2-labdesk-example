# Project brief: LabDesk

> Worked example for teaching (LabDesk). Not a student submission and not a real product.

Lessons 1 and 2 (kick-off, project management, handover from the client). Every later document
refers back to the names fixed here.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Product | LabDesk, a seat reservation system for the faculty computer labs |
| Client | Faculty office that runs the computer labs (example client) |
| Client contact | Head of the lab unit (example) |
| Team | Six members, Owner A to Owner F |
| Course | DBIZ2 Digital Business Project II, followed by DBIZ3 (build) |

## Problem

Students sign up for lab seats on a paper sheet at the lab door. Popular slots fill in minutes,
students who booked often do not come, and their seats stay empty while others queue outside. Lab
Technicians learn about faulty computers only when a student complains, and the faculty has no
data on how the labs are used.

## Goal of the project

Students book a seat before they come, seats that nobody uses are given back quickly, and Lab
Technicians know which seats are out of order. Measures agreed with the client are in
[the CVCA](../context/cvca.md) (stakeholder requirements) and, in money terms, in
[W6 cash flow](../function-list/w6-cash-flow.md).
